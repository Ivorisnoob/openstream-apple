# Changelog

## 0.1 (November 2026)

The first version of OpenStream for Mac.

- Home, Movies, TV Shows and Anime with featured titles and shelves; title, person, genre and search pages
- Streams from Stremio add-ons, web players, Re:Anime and Anikoto, searched together and ranked; failover at the same spot; the last working source asked first
- Apple's player in its own window, with Picture in Picture, AirPlay and full screen
- DASH streams played as HLS
- Subtitles from OpenSubtitles and the sources, styled in Settings
- An Audio menu for the stream's tracks and other languages across sources
- Skip Intro and Skip Recap for anime, and Up Next when the credits start
- Continue Watching, Watchlist, History, and library backup and restore
- Settings for appearance, playback, quality, audio language, subtitles, library and storage
